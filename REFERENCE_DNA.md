# Chronosword's Last Day — reference DNA

This is the taste map for the jam build. It is not a list of surfaces to copy.
Each reference has one job. If a feature cannot name the job it is doing, it
does not belong in the game.

Chronosword is neither project in miniature. It borrows their conviction that
mobility, fragility, supernatural competence, and story can occupy the same
button press.

## What each cluster contributes

### Hands, speed, and authored mastery

Bayonetta, Ninja Gaiden, Team Ladybug, The Game Bakers, Copy Kitty, fighting
games, and shmups supply the combat standard:

- a complete late-game moveset from the first input;
- enemies that ask different questions rather than differing only in health;
- animation anticipation, immediate contact, readable recovery, and useful
  cancels;
- power expressed through precision while the protagonist remains fragile;
- boss patterns that become legible and satisfying under pressure.

The target is not a giant combo list. The dagger, absent-dagger fists, kick,
deflection, Chronostep, Watchfire, and dagger path must cross-connect so a small
kit produces expert decisions.

### Traversal that is part of combat

Sonic, EXIT PATH, Ori, Titanfall 2, Sands of Time, Rayman Legends, VVVVVV, and
The End Is Nigh supply the movement standard:

- intent reaches velocity quickly;
- the camera acknowledges motion without oscillating after the player has made
  a clean decision;
- jumps, slides, wall-kicks, and short time-steps preserve routes through a
  fight;
- a skilled line is faster, safer, and more expressive, not merely decorative.

### Compact games that remain in the hands

Slayin, Osmos, Knightmare Tower, Super Hexagon, mobile games, and Flash games
supply compactness:

- the premise is understood within seconds;
- retry friction is close to zero;
- one unusual rule reshapes every ordinary action;
- visual feedback is immediate enough that play feels good before content
  quantity can carry it.

Rogue Legacy, Isaac, Spelunky, Dicey Dungeons, Don't Starve, and related run
games contribute pressure and replay texture, but **not** a full-game time loop
or roguelite progression layer.

### Sincere worlds with abrasive edges

Lain, vibingleaf, Watch Dogs 1, The Desolate Hope, Picayune Dreams, Post Void,
Undertale, Type:Rider, RuneScape, Don't Starve, Shovel Knight, and lost-media
memory supply the atmosphere:

- familiar infrastructure is haunted by specific history;
- interface fragments can carry story without becoming a visual novel;
- strange imagery is emotionally sincere, not an ironic lore dump;
- the game should feel like an overlooked, technically overcommitted artifact
  from its era;
- nightmare logic is authored into places, enemies, and rules instead of
  sprayed on top as VHS, backrooms, posterization, or constant RGB split.

## The Aesthetic Battle Simulator lesson

*Aesthetic Battle Simulator* was a delisted 2019 mobile runner-shooter by Semyon
Kozyura. Surviving store archives describe a deliberately excessive mixture of
classical statuary, 1990s/internet imagery, absurd weapons, and psychedelic
locations. Its useful lesson is **not** its vaporwave palette, memes, or
constant flashing. The useful lesson is that its aesthetic lives in every
gameplay noun: enemy, projectile, pickup, weapon, backdrop, and transition.

Chronosword applies that lesson in its own restrained language:

- a melee enemy is an old rail collector returning to finish a committed cut;
- a ranged enemy is a signal witness whose body frames a broken clock face;
- its projectiles are stamped timetable seals and signal vanes, not yellow
  blobs;
- an elite is a buried post-game retainer with a segmented guard;
- the final unfinished thing rises through ballast and asphalt;
- hits leave rail sparks, ash, watch-hand echoes, or scraps of punched tickets;
- quiet traversal stays quiet so the supernatural actions have contrast.

The original game is no longer available through its first-party store listing,
so details beyond its identity and broad runner-shooter structure are
archive-derived. Useful research starting points:

- developer release post:
  <https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedevscreens/comments/c0kdzi>
- archived App Store metadata:
  <https://appshunter.io/ios/app/aesthetic-battle-simulator/id1373005563>

## Visual law

### Palette

- soot: `#151715`
- wet asphalt: `#252824`
- old concrete: `#777363`
- rail steel: `#77756A`
- oxidized iron: `#6E3820`
- dead paper: `#D0C6A2`
- wound rust: `#7A2018`
- Watchfire shadow: `#32123F`
- Watchfire hot core: `#AA73BC`

Purple is restricted to the chronosword's time art. Rust red belongs to trauma
and the returning past. Warm ivory belongs to readable hit confirmation and
the watch. None is a global neon grade.

### Surface

Use continuous color, rough materials, grime breakup, silhouettes, fog, bloom
on genuinely overbright effects, and selective temporal smearing. Do not use
PS1 quantization, affine wobble, a permanent glitch filter, scanlines, or a
backrooms-yellow wash.

Browser builds use Godot's Compatibility renderer. “HDR” therefore means an
HDR internal lighting pipeline, Filmic tonemapping, overbright emissive accents,
and restrained glow; web output itself is SDR.

## Smoothness is a contract

Smoothness does not mean slowly lerping everything.

- **Intent:** grounded directional input should materially change velocity
  within about 80 ms.
- **Camera:** a strafe lean should acknowledge input within one or two rendered
  frames and settle in roughly 120 ms, with no oscillation when direction is
  held and no overshoot after reversal.
- **Hands:** locomotion offsets follow the stable movement intent. Repeated
  footfall motion is primarily vertical and primarily caused by forward travel;
  pure strafing must not swing both hands left-right.
- **Actions:** jump, strike contact, kick, throw, deflect, and Watchfire
  activation happen immediately when their committed input window begins.
- **Follow-through:** recovery, recoil, flame lag, damage-number drift, and
  environmental response can ease after the action. They may never delay the
  action itself.
- **Time bending:** hostile animation and projectiles slow, while the broken
  watch's seconds hand accelerates and leaves short analog hand echoes. This
  contradiction is the unmistakable ability read.

## Eight-hour priority

1. A short authored train-ride/crash/recovery opening that hands control back
   quickly.
2. Stable movement and camera response.
3. Distinct enemy entrances, silhouettes, and projectile grammars.
4. Second hand, Watchfire acceleration, temporal smear, impact punctuation.
5. Surface breakup and wreck dressing from licensed assets.
6. Tight encounter timing, damage numbers, audio hooks, reduced-flash option.
7. User-facing swap points for final art and music.

Anything below this list is cut before these promises are weakened.
